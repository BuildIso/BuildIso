Email Communication
Overview
Email is a critical communication channel for BuildIso Projects. Users can submit bug reports, feature requests, and feedback directly to our team.

Contact Information
Send emails to:

BuildIso@proton.me - General inquiries and support
noreply@buildiso.com - Account notifications and OTP emails
Email Guidelines
To help us assist you effectively, please include:

Your BuildIso Projects version number
A clear subject line
Detailed description of your issue or request
Steps to reproduce any bugs (if applicable)
Response Time
Our team aims to respond promptly. Response times may vary based on volume and issue complexity.

Security
Important: We use Proton Mail for privacy and security. Proton Mail offers end-to-end encryption and operates under Swiss privacy laws.

Fake Email Warnings
Be cautious of emails falsely claiming to be from BuildIso. Always verify the sender's email address. If you receive a suspicious email:

Do not click suspicious links or download attachments
Report it immediately to BuildIso@proton.me
Unsubscribe Options
To unsubscribe from our mailing list, look for the unsubscribe link in any email.

Note: Do not unsubscribe from noreply@buildiso.com if you want to receive account OTP emails.
