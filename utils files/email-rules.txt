Rules for Submitting an Email to BuildIso@proton.me or noreply@buildiso.com
Rules
Rules for submitting an email to BuildIso@proton.me or noreply@buildiso.com:

Use a clear and concise subject line that summarizes the purpose of your email.
Include a polite greeting and introduction in the body of the email.
Clearly state the reason for contacting BuildIso and provide any relevant details or information.
Be respectful and professional in your language and tone throughout the email.
Avoid using slang, offensive language, or making inappropriate requests.
Include any necessary attachments or links to support your email, if applicable.
End the email with a polite closing and your contact information for follow-up, if necessary.
Remember that BuildIso Team may receive a high volume of emails, so it may take some time for them to respond. Be patient and avoid sending multiple follow-up emails unless it is necessary.
By following these rules, you can help ensure that your email is well-received and increases the likelihood of a positive response from the BuildIso Team.
