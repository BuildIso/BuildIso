Rules of Contributing
What are the rules of contributing to BuildIso?
When contributing to BuildIso, it's important to follow these rules:

Respect the official team and their decisions.
Do not contribute to unofficial teams without permission.
Ensure your contributions are original and do not violate any licenses.
Follow the guidelines set forth by the official BuildIso Team.
Be respectful and collaborative with other contributors.
Do not share or distribute unofficial versions of BuildIso without permission.
Report any issues or bugs to the official BuildIso Team.
Do not use the BuildIso name or logo for unofficial projects without permission.
Do not distribute or promote any unauthorized modifications of BuildIso.
Learn about contributing

For more information about contributing to BuildIso, please refer to the Contributing page.
