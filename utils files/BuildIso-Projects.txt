BuildIso Projects
What are BuildIso Projects?
BuildIso Projects includes all repositories of BuildIso (doesn't include BuildIso Pro). It contains: BuildIso, BuildIso Defender, BuildIso Encrypt, CXWNetwork, ripzip, and pkgdb.

How to install BuildIso Projects?
Launch SetupBuildIsoProjects.exe, then click "I agree" → "Next" → "Install" → "Wait" → "Finish"

Where are the executables located after installing?
All executables are located in C:\BuildIsoProjects (default path).

Why is BuildIso Projects large?
BuildIso Projects is large because it includes all repositories of BuildIso (except BuildIso Pro), which includes: BuildIso, BuildIso Defender, BuildIso Encrypt, CXWNetwork, ripzip, and pkgdb.

What are the system requirements?
The requirements for BuildIso Projects are as follows:

Windows 11
560 MB of available disk space
Is BuildIso Projects free?
Yes, BuildIso Projects is free because it doesn't include BuildIso Pro.
